Test protocol

  • Split by source, never by frame or image. Each source (a person or a video) lives in exactly one of train or test. The data loader refuses a manifest where a source appears in both.
  • Seen vs. unseen fakes. A fake type present in training is “seen”. A type absent from training is “unseen”. The unseen column is the one to read: it approximates a new generator.
  • False positives on real content. The share of real test images wrongly flagged is a headline metric, because accusing a real photo is the costly mistake.
  • Fixed threshold, no tuning on test. The judge's decision point is calibrated on training data to flag about 10% of real training images, then evaluated on the test split unchanged.
  • Robustness. The same test images are re-scored after JPEG compression and downscaling, to see what survives real-world sharing.

Robustness to compression

Messaging apps, social media and screenshots recompress and resize images. We re-scored every test image after each of these steps, using the same trained models.

Limitations

1

It misses many fakes by design.The judge is tuned to rarely accuse real content, which costs recall. See the detection rate above: a “likely real” verdict is not a certificate of authenticity.

2

Small, narrow training data.A few thousand face images from a handful of generators. Real photos from other cameras, ages, lighting or demographics may behave differently, and we have not measured performance across demographic groups.

3

New generators are the hard case.Detectors trained on older generators degrade on newer ones. Only one fake type here is truly unseen, so treat unseen performance as a weak signal, not a guarantee.

4

Compression erodes the evidence.The frequency cue in particular fades under JPEG and resizing, and ELA turns itself off on recompressed files.

5

Metadata is easy to strip or forge.It nudges the score slightly. C2PA content credentials are detected as present but not yet cryptographically verified.

6

Faces only, and only simply.Detection uses a Haar-cascade face finder with a centre-crop fallback, so profile, small or occluded faces may be missed. Multi-face images are not scored per face.

7

Video and audio are shallower than images, and unmeasured.Every number on this page is for still images. Video samples 16 frames with no temporal deepfake model and no blink or pulse analysis. The lip-sync check is a training-free heuristic we have not yet evaluated on labelled video. Audio needs a trained checkpoint.

8

It does not say which generator, or prove intent.The output is evidence about pixels and sound, not about who made something or why.

Responsible use

  • Don't use this alone to accuse anyone. A probabilistic score should start a human investigation, not end one. Treat low-confidence results as unanswered.
  • Consent and privacy. Analysis runs on your machine and uploads are kept in a temporary folder. Still, only examine content you have the right to examine.
  • Test fakes. Only create test fakes with your own face and voice, or with licensed data.
  • Detectors go out of date. A published detector can be studied and evaded, so re-run the evaluation regularly and keep an unseen-generator test.
